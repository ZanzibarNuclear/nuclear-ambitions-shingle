# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Nuclear Ambitions LLC ("we" or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information that we may obtain through your use of Nuclear Ambitions digital properties (the "Platform").

<!--@include: ../parts/properties.md-->

## 1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide: When you use the Platform, you may voluntarily provide personal information, such as your name, email address, and account credentials when registering for an account or subscribing to our newsletter.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information: We collect certain information automatically when you visit the Platform, including:

- Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
- Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on the Platform, and interactions with content.
  We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect this information (see Section 3).
- Children’s Data: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we learn that we have collected such data without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.
- Sensitive Data: We do not collect sensitive personal information, such as payment details or health information, unless explicitly stated.

## 2. Use of Information

2.1 We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain the Platform, including account management and user support.
- To improve the Platform's functionality and user experience, such as through analytics.
- To communicate with you, including responding to inquiries and sending newsletters (if you subscribe).
- To comply with legal obligations or protect our legal rights.

<!-- -->

2.2 Third-Party Sharing: We may share your information with service providers (e.g., hosting providers, analytics tools) to operate the Platform. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.

2.3 Legal Basis (EU Users): We process your data based on your consent in order to operate Platform services in a personalized fashion for you.

## 3. Cookies

3.1 We use cookies and similar technologies (e.g., web beacons, pixels) to enhance your experience on the Platform. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us:

- Ensure the Platform functions properly (essential cookies).
- Provide information for access control and prevention of use by automated bots (session cookies).
- Analyze Platform usage to improve performance (analytics cookies).
- Personalize content or ads, if applicable (marketing cookies).

<!-- -->

3.2 You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie consent tool or by adjusting your browser settings to refuse cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect the Platform's functionality.

## 4. Third-Party Links

4.1 The Platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of these websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information. Accessing third-party links is at your own risk.

4.2 Affiliate Links: If we include affiliate links, we may earn a commission for purchases made through these links, at no additional cost to you.

## 5. Data Security

5.1 We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as encryption and access controls, to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Additional Rights

6.1 You may have additional rights depending on your jurisdiction. Feel free to contact us about any data and privacy concerns. You are also free to delete your account, and we will remove or, where removal is detrimental to system operations, anonymize your data.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

7.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on the Platform. We will make no attempt to notify you of these changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically if you are so inclined.
